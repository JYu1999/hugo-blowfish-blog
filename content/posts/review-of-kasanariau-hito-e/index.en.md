Actually, I don't have too many thoughts. Overall, it's an easy and enjoyable read.

When I first started reading this book, I was a bit confused because the names appeared somewhat abruptly, so I couldn't figure out who was who.

Also, it's written from the perspectives of two protagonists, so halfway through you'll have a "huh?" feeling XD, but once you figure out the characters later on, it becomes much smoother.

I really like the narrative style in the book, where small details from the present evoke past memories, and then carefully reflect on the feelings at that time.

Furthermore, as the story progresses, I kept looking forward to the subsequent developments of the main and supporting characters, as well as those unrevealed past events.

When I finished the last page, I suddenly realized there was no more. I thought my e-book format had messed up, but after checking, I found out it really only goes this far.

Looking forward to the next volume~!
