I visited the bookstore the other day and stumbled upon this book.

The book band featured some hyperbolic taglines, such as: "Absolutely impossible to publish as an ebook—a moving experience only possible with a physical book," and "Taiwan is likely the only place in the world capable of perfectly translating this novel." Naturally, this piqued my curiosity.

I bought it, took it home, and finished it quickly.

## The plot is slightly regrettable

Basically, this book is a mystery novel. I thought the initial setup was pretty good; it made me think along with the protagonist: "Okay, so what is the answer?"

However, I personally didn't care for the way the mystery was revealed—it felt a bit anticlimactic.

Furthermore, the character development wasn't very deep. Many characters felt like bit players or seemed to exist solely for the sake of existing.

The book also didn't provide much detail on certain points at the end.

My feeling after finishing it was: "Oh, so that's all it was."

I wouldn't call it bad, but it felt quite flat.

## Impossible to publish as an ebook???

Actually, I figured out why about halfway through... LOL.

I felt the hints were too obvious. Also, I'm not sure if it's because this is a translated version, but when I discovered the trick, I wasn't particularly amazed.

Moreover, to accommodate this "mystery," I feel sacrifices were made regarding the text and punctuation. This made the reading experience feel a bit jarring to me in the beginning.

## Is this book completely worthless?

To me, I consider this book a work full of "craftsmanship."

You can genuinely feel how much effort went into bringing this book to life.

And through the explanations within the story, I was able to learn: "Oh, so novelists consider things like this when they are creating."

So, it was still a fruitful experience~!

However, I think the marketing slogans were too exaggerated. They raised my expectations too high, leading to a strong sense of letdown after finishing it.

Then again, if the marketing hadn't been so over-the-top, I might not have bought it in the first place (?
