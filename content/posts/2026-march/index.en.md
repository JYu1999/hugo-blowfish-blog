## Let's start with some reflection...

### Interviews

In last month's review, I mentioned wanting to continue interviewing, but looking around this month, nothing really caught my interest, so I didn't send out my resume.

Thinking about it carefully, my current job is actually very close to my ideal state, including the industry, job content, working style, company culture, team atmosphere, etc.

Additionally, the company is still growing, so there will continuously be new challenges and opportunities, meaning it won't feel boring.

I briefly reviewed the three motives I had for wanting to start interviewing:

1. I feel like I've been at this company for quite a while, and my technical learning seems to have decreased, so I want to try a new environment.
2. To practice interview skills for potential dream jobs that might appear in the future. Like how to market myself, showcase my skills, and answer technical interview questions.
3. Understand market rates to avoid getting tied down to a single company.

I realized the first point is no longer valid. In fact, I later discovered that unless a company has completely stopped growing, there should always be plenty to do from a technical perspective.

I forgot where I heard this view, but it goes: **It's not that there's no demand, it's just that people are concerned about capacity, so they don't bring it up.**

If you look carefully, you can always find shortcomings in existing products, team processes, etc., and try to solve them.

The reason I used to feel bored was probably because I limited myself to being a "Backend Engineer," only thinking about doing backend-related things. (Hopefully not jinxing myself here 😂)

In January, I mentioned the concept of "Fake it till you make it," which means if I want to become a certain role, I just treat my current self as "already being it," and I can make judgments directly from that role's perspective.

When I placed myself in the roles of SRE Leader, Engineering Manager, AI Engineer, etc., I found that the things I could do were piled as high as a mountain XD

The needs for the second and third points still exist, but for me right now, they don't seem urgent, so taking it slow is fine.

I will probably still browse around occasionally in the future, but again, I'll only apply if something truly interests me.
