Another year has come to an end, and it's time to reflect on some things I've learned this year.

- Focus on yourself, clarify your emotions, vision, and essence. Only when you know what you truly want can you know how to act and how to interact comfortably with others.
- A blessing in disguise. "Facts" can often be interpreted from many angles, and many things only become clear over the long term, including investment strategies, knowledge accumulation, and relationships. So there's no need to rush to conclusions about single events.
- Improve your abilities and manage your expectations and desires. Much dissatisfaction and irrationality stem from abilities not keeping up with desires.
- Rather than pursuing success and wealth, I yearn more for "peace." Not the peace of giving up, but the peace of being able to control my life and my mind, unaffected by other people or things.
- Time is risk, so don't delay things that can be done quickly. (But that doesn't mean taking reckless risks)
- The world changes too fast; perhaps those things that don't change are the most important.
- From "The Almanack of Naval Ravikant": You always have three choices: change it, accept it, or leave it. The worst thing is to do nothing, hoping for change but not acting, knowing you can't leave but unable to accept it.

Actually, the most memorable feedback I received this year wasn't about my abilities, mindset, or external conditions, but rather:

> I think you have a genuine side, which is quite uncommon, and it's different from being naive. This trait can help you gain the trust of both strong people and good people.

I'm not sure why this feedback left such a deep impression on me. I'm still pondering it... XD
