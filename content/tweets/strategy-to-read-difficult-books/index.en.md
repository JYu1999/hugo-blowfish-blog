## How to read difficult books?

To talk about how to use AI for assistance, we first need to understand the normal process.

Without AI, how do you read difficult books?

After a quick survey, I saw an article: [《how to read difficult books》](https://ranahabib.substack.com/p/how-to-read-difficult-books).

Here is a brief summary of the content that is helpful to me:

![如何閱讀困難書籍](/tweets/strategy-to-read-difficult-books/image.png)

### Multiple Reading Method

For difficult texts, the author proposes a "Multiple Reading Method".

The author argues that "a book should not be read only once", but rather through layered reading steps, from initial contact to deep analysis, to truly digest the content.

So how do you proceed with the "Multiple Reading Method"?

#### Phase 1: Familiarity and Marking

Don't over-analyze during the first reading; the goal is to increase familiarity with the content.

Just read and mark interesting parts with tags or highlighters.

#### Phase 2: Deconstruction and Connection

Use margin notes to deconstruct the key points of the first pass.

Try to restate key concepts, draw connections between viewpoints, and even write down opposing opinions.

#### Phase 3: External Assistance

If the text is still obscure and difficult to understand, pause reading and turn to YouTube videos, documentaries, or Podcasts to get summaries or background knowledge, and then return to reading with new understanding.

### Writing as a Learning Tool

Just like my original intention for writing this blog, the author also believes that output is key to internalizing knowledge.

The author suggests writing a 1-2 page summary or even publishing a book review after reading and marking key points.

During the reading process, you should also note down concepts you want to study further at any time; after reading, rely on previous markings rather than rereading the whole book to write a summary.

## Combining with AI

Ok, now I have a preliminary concept of how to read difficult books, so how should I use AI to make this faster, more convenient, and make my learning deeper?

Here is my initial strategy:

### 1. Skimming Content

In the article above, the author mentioned that you can read the article completely once first, with the goal of getting familiar with the content.

So now at the end of 2025, what should be done to better achieve this goal?

I will choose to use NotebookLM to generate Chinese videos and slides for each chapter. This has several benefits:

1. Use AI to help me quickly find key points
2. Chinese absorption speed is better than English, after all, Chinese is my mother tongue
3. Watching videos and slides is more relaxing than reading text

After watching the videos and slides, I will have a moderate understanding of the content, and then I can enter the second stage.

### 2. Detailed Reading

In this stage, I will start to read the content of the book in detail.

At this time, I will start to mark some key points and create concept cards in Heptabase so that each concept can be related.

However, if I encounter questions or content that I want to study in depth, I will choose to mark it first. The purpose is to ensure a complete reading experience. Otherwise, if I am often distracted to study other things during the reading process, I may not be able to establish the correlation of the book content well.

### 3. Quiz

Ok, at this time I have finished reading a chapter, it is time to test my familiarity with the chapter.

At this time, I will use NotebookLM to create questions based on the content of the chapter. I will then actually do the questions to see if there are any misconceptions or unfamiliar parts.

If so, I will figure out the unfamiliar parts.

### 4. Deep Dive

In this stage, I already have a certain familiarity with the content of the book, it is time to deal with the content marked in the second stage.

I will pick out the marked content one by one and conduct in-depth research, discussion, and even implementation.

### 5. Output

After the previous steps, I should be very familiar with the content of the chapter at this time. It's time to start the final integration and output it as an article.

In the process of outputting, I may find that I can't explain it very clearly. This is another opportunity to review or figure it out.
