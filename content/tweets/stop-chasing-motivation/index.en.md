In recent months, whenever I wanted to study software technology seriously, I would suddenly feel a bit disinterested.

Then I would tell myself: "Well, maybe software isn't my passion; I should find something else to learn."

As a result, I didn't find any other passions to learn either, and time was wasted for nothing.

This short video reminded me that passion comes from discipline, not something that can be "found".
