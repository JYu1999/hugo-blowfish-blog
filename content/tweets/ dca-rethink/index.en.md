DCA has been an investment method I've used for a long time.

Since I got my first salary after entering the workforce, I started my DCA journey.

However, I never really thought deeply about why DCA works, what situations are suitable for DCA, etc.

I agree with the viewpoints mentioned in this video.

DCA should focus on assets that have "stood the test of time," such as gold, ETFs, etc., so that we can expect long-term upward trends.

If you DCA assets that are still proving themselves, you're essentially gambling.
