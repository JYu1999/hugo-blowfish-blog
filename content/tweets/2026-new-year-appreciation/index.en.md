I think I'm a lucky person.

Although I sometimes encounter annoying things, 99% of the people around me are good people.

I'm very grateful that these people have appeared in my life, and I sincerely hope everyone can become better and become who they want to be.

For the new year, I want to start with some positive energy, so here's a gratitude post~~

## Manager

A few days ago, I had a 1-on-1 with my manager and mentioned that I wanted to start job hunting recently.

Before bringing it up, I actually hesitated a bit, because not everyone has this kind of mindset and perspective.

Later, I thought that I've been working with this manager for a while, and we probably know each other's personalities, so it should be fine.

What surprised me was that my manager's response was very supportive, and even proactively thought about what help I might need.

I'm really grateful.

## Muay Thai Gym Coach

A few days ago, I gave the coach some desserts my girlfriend made.

The coach proactively told me that if we open a shop in the future, we could consider working with them for customer acquisition.

I was really touched when I heard that.

Afterwards, both the coach and his girlfriend sent messages saying they really liked the desserts my girlfriend made.

I'm really lucky to have found this Muay Thai gym.
