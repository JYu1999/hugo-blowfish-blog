Whenever I want to buy something that's not a daily necessity, I tell myself to wait a week and come back to it.

After a week, I tell myself to wait another week.

Usually, I end up not spending the money.

This method has saved me at least hundreds of thousands, and it works every time XD

Just like what's mentioned in *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant: A Guide to Wealth and Happiness*:

> "If you can't decide, the answer is no."

If you're not sure whether to buy something, the answer is usually "don't buy it."

But my thinking did a complete 180-degree turn last night.

Last night I was very tired and wanted to sleep quickly. But because I accidentally drank too much water before bed, I really needed to pee less than half an hour after falling asleep.

But I really wanted to sleep, so I tried to hold it in and force myself to fall asleep.

After another hour, I still got up to pee.

When I got up, I suddenly had a realization and wrote this article.

Many things can be clarified with time, but needing to pee in the middle of the night isn't one of them.

When you need to pee, you just have to pee.

Okay, this article is really pointless XD
