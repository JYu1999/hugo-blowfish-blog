I just received the confirmation of the refund from Emirates.

Fortunately, we got back about 60-70% of our expenses. I haven't calculated the exact amount in detail.

When we saw Iran bombing everywhere, my girlfriend and I seriously discussed whether to take a gamble and go, or wait for an official announcement from Emirates.

We finally decided to ask for a refund first. After all, the waiting process was too agonizing, refreshing the Emirates website all day and checking others' latest refund progress.

Moreover, the variables of war are too huge. Even if it seems peaceful now, a terrorist organization might suddenly emerge.

After deciding to refund, it was a series of tedious processes, including Emirates, Aegean Airlines, booking websites, and so on.

I had to figure out every platform. For Aegean Airlines, I even had to call them personally to cancel. It was really quite troublesome.

---

Actually, this isn't our first time failing to go to Greece. We had another failed attempt last year because of flight rescheduling, causing our flight times to not connect. We had no choice but to change our itinerary to Thailand.

After these two times, I've actually started to feel somewhat resistant towards Greece, but since it's the place my girlfriend wants to visit the most, we'll probably find another time to go eventually.

---

Of course, the important thing is what to do next~

I just happened to see Takeru's retirement fight ONE SAMURAI 1 against Rodtang, so we decided to change our original Greece trip into a Japan trip 😂

After discussing it with my girlfriend, we'll probably go to Japan as digital nomads for a month, traveling from Aomori all the way to Tokyo, and dropping by to see the currently very popular Japanese baby macaque Panchi (パンチ).

My girlfriend said my smile was noticeably brighter when booking the ONE tickets than when booking the tickets to Greece... I don't think so 😆
