During the Lunar New Year holiday this year, I traveled to Tokyo with my family. Since I had some free time, I decided to experience a local Muay Thai gym.

At the time, this was the closest gym to our accommodation that accepted foreigners:

Overall, I thought it was really good!

When you first go in, the instructor asks you to change clothes and warm up on your own.

The scheduled class time was 19:00, but when I finished my warm-up around 19:15, there was only one other student, and the instructor hadn't officially come out yet.

Anyway, the instructor seemed to ask that student to hold pads for me, and during this time, other people started trickling in.

The official class started around 20:00.

The class was divided into pad work and sparring.

For the pad work, the instructor taught some simple combinations, and the students held the pads for each other.

The explanation of the movements wasn't as detailed as at my gym in Taiwan; it was just a typical gym style.

After that, it was crazy light sparring: 3 rounds of pure boxing + 3 rounds of Muay Thai + 1 round with the instructor.

While sparring, I found that the other students were generally very strong, and there were a few competitors who absolutely crushed me XD.

At the very end, there was a little bit of conditioning: sit-ups, kicking the heavy bag, etc.

The fee was 3300 JPY, which I consider worth it.

The environment was just a typical gym environment 😂
