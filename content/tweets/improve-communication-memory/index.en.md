Lately I've been doing interviews. After rewatching my own answers, I noticed two weaknesses I keep ignoring. I want to record them here as my focus for this year.

## 1. Communication & expression

When an interviewer asks a question, I often loop around and end up saying the same thing.

- I don't hit the point clearly
- I repeat the same two or three sentences
- My answer isn't convincing enough

Looking back, there were better ways to say it. But in the moment, maybe because of nerves, I just couldn't get it out.

**This is a major improvement target for me this year.**

I don't have a perfect solution yet, but I'll start with:

- Doing more interviews to get used to being questioned
- Watching how others answer and learning the difference
- Practicing "getting to the point" in work and daily life

## 2. Memory

My memory is honestly not great. A day or two after a discussion, I often forget what we talked about, or the concrete conclusions and details.

I do take notes, but I realized note-taking can distract me, and I end up not fully listening.

So I want to adjust two things:

1. **Learn to capture only the truly important points**
   - Don't write a lot without focus
   - Practice listening while still keeping the conversation flowing
2. **Try a transcription workflow**
   - Record the meeting
   - Use a transcription tool to generate the full text
   - Use AI to summarize the key points

Why not use Slack? I find its transcription inaccurate, and recording there makes conversations feel unnatural.

So I want to try my own setup and see if it feels better.

## Closing

Writing these two weaknesses down is my reminder: **this year I want to practice not just skills, but also expression and memory.**

Hopefully by the end of the year, I can say I got a little better.
