Since ChatGPT came out in 2022, discussions about AI have been endless:
- "Will AI replace software engineers?"
- "In the AI era, what professions will be replaced first?"
- "After AI appears, the first to be eliminated will be those who don't learn AI."

So, what is it that AI cannot replace?

I think, in the end, it's the relationships between people, love and hate.

I am very lucky to live in an era witnessing the rapid development of AI, to see with my own eyes that when those hard skills are gradually replaced, we can finally re-examine what is truly important to us.

I also hope that you who see this article have your own answer.
